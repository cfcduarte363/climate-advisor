Drop an EPW weather file here

An EnergyPlus Weather file holds 8 760 hourly records — one for every hour of a representative year — describing temperature, humidity, solar radiation, wind and sky conditions at a specific place on Earth. This tool reads the file entirely in your browser and turns it into charts you can read, interrogate and export.

Read the year at a glance

Every variable in the file is plotted as an annual carpet — 365 days across, 24 hours up — so the shape of the climate, its seasons and its daily rhythm become a single image. Sunrise and sunset are drawn over it.

Design from the psychrometric chart

The full year is projected onto a psychrometric chart with the Givoni–Milne building bioclimatic zones. The percentage of hours falling in each zone tells you which passive strategies actually matter for this site.

Wind, sun and comfort

Wind roses by season and hour, a stereographic sun path with the year's temperatures mapped onto it, adaptive comfort bands to EN 16798-1, and degree days. Every chart exports to PNG or CSV.

Nothing is uploaded. The file never leaves this computer.

Do not have a weather file yet?

Weather files for several thousand stations worldwide are published through Ladybug Tools EPWMap. Open the map, find the station nearest your site, download the .epw, and drop it above. Prefer a station at a comparable altitude and distance from the coast over one that is merely closer in kilometres — in Portugal, Bragança and Faro are 500 km apart and belong to different worlds.
